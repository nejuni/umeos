# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

As contributors and maintainers of Juni, we are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback graciously and explain disagreements respectfully.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else’s personal information without permission.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, insults, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces. Do not spam, impersonate maintainers, or misrepresent unofficial builds as official Juni releases.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They will consider the context and impact of each incident and aim to respond fairly.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to **Sharavan Kumar** at <ume@aster.cx>. Include a description of what happened and any relevant links or context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
